Usage guidance

Suitable for
  • Research on brain ageing within a single acquisition site
  • Comparing groups scanned on the same scanner and protocol
  • Teaching and methods development
Not suitable for
  • Any clinical or individual decision
  • Comparing brain-age gaps across scanners without site-aware validation
  • Uncertainty intervals that are not calibrated per site

Known limitations

  • IOP has 61 subjects; the offset replicates in validation, test, 5-fold CV and three training seeds (−5.2 to −6.2).
  • In IXI, site is confounded with who each site recruited: the cause of the IOP offset (untested scanner properties or cohort) cannot be separated.
  • Healthy volunteers aged 20–86 only. Attribution resolution 8 mm. Uncertainty is the model's own spread, not a calibrated interval.
