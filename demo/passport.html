Suitable for

  • Research on brain ageing within a single acquisition site
  • Comparing groups scanned on the same scanner and protocol
  • Teaching and methods development

Not suitable for

  • Any clinical or individual decision
  • Comparing brain-age gaps across scanners without site-aware validation
  • New sites, without checking their uncertainty first

Known limitations

01Small subgroups (9 IOP test subjects); single seed and split. The IOP offset still replicates in validation (−4.1) and test (−5.1).
02In IXI, site is confounded with who each site recruited: the cause of the IOP offset (untested scanner properties or cohort) cannot be separated.
03Healthy volunteers aged 20–86 only. Attribution resolution 8 mm. Uncertainty is the model’s own spread, not a calibrated interval.
